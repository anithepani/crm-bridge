Rules
01. Who can take part
● Open to everyone. Students, graduates and working professionals are all welcome.
● Teams are 1 or 2 people. No exceptions, and no swapping members after check-in.
● Every team that registers confirms their team at the registration desk on 19 September.
● A person can be on one team only.
● Teams who attended the live session on 18th September, and made some progress.
(mandatory)
02. Before you arrive
● Attend the online session on 18 September. Your Fastn account must be created and
upgraded before the on-site day, and you can start building as soon as it ends.
● Bring your own laptop and charger. Working internet is provided at the venue, but a
mobile hotspot as backup is a good idea.
● Create the accounts you plan to connect in advance, and prepare test data in them. This
is allowed and encouraged.
● Reading the docs, sketching your idea, choosing your track and building your workflow
ahead of the on-site day are all allowed.
03. What you build
● Pick one of the four tracks in the hackathon brief, or propose your own use case under
the theme.
● The build must run on Fastn: connectors, triggers and workflows in your own Fastn
workspace.
● The automation has to connect at least two systems and move real data between them,
end to end.
● A mockup, a slide deck or a design with no working workflow behind it will not be scored
on implementation.
04. The build window
● You can start building from 18 September. There is no restriction on when you begin.
● Connectors, triggers, workflows, widgets and code can all be set up before the on-site
day. Come in with as much progress as you like.
● Building ends at 4:30 PM on 19 September, when the submission form closes.
● Mentors are available on the floor through the build day on 19 September.
05. Help you can use
● Mentors are on the floor all day. Ask them anything.
● The Fastn Platform Agent, the documentation and AI coding assistants are all allowed,

and using the Platform Agent well is worth 20 points.
● Open-source libraries and public templates are fine as long as your submission is your
own work.
● Taking another team's workflow or submission is not, and means disqualification.
06. Submitting
● A submission form is provided on the day. Everything goes through that form, which
closes at 4:30 PM sharp.
● Your demo video must show the automation actually working, not slides about it.
● Set your Google Drive video link so that anyone with the link can view it. A link nobody
can open counts as a missing video.
● You can submit more than once. Only your last submission before the deadline is judged.
● Late submissions are not judged, whatever the reason.
07. Feedback form — mandatory
● Every participant is given a feedback form on the day. Filling it is compulsory.
● A team is not considered for any prize until every member has submitted the feedback
form.
● It takes a couple of minutes. Do it before the closing ceremony so your team stays
eligible.
08. Judging
● There is no live demo. Judges score what you submit against the 100-point rubric below.
● Judges may call a team over to ask a question about their submission.
● If two teams tie, the higher Idea and innovation score wins. If that ties too, the higher
Platform Agent score wins.
● The judges' decision is final.
09. Conduct
● Treat every participant, mentor, judge and organiser with respect. Harassment of any kind
means removal from the event.
● Follow NUST campus rules and the instructions of the SEECS staff and organising team.
● Keep your workspace clean and leave the hall as you found it.
● Organisers may disqualify any team that breaks these rules.
10. Prizes and swag
● First place PKR 150,000, second place PKR 100,000, third place PKR 50,000.
● Prizes are handed over at the closing ceremony on 19 September.
● The feedback form must be submitted by every team member before prizes are awarded.
● Swag is distributed to participants on-site on 19 September.

Use-cases

What Is Fastn?
Fastn is an embedded integrations platform.
Normally, if a SaaS product wants to connect to other tools (like Salesforce, Slack, or Shopify), its engineering team
has to build and maintain a separate integration for each one. An embedded integration platform changes that. It lets
a SaaS product offer integrations natively, inside its own product, instead of sending customers to a separate tool like
Zapier or Make. Fastn handles the connectors, authentication, and data mapping inside the SaaS company's own UI.
The engineering team builds the integration once and embeds it in the product. After that, each customer can set up
their own connections themselves, without any extra engineering work.
How Different SaaS Businesses Use It
• A vertical CRM adds an “Integrations” tab in its own settings where customers connect their email, calendar,
or accounting tools themselves, without filing a support ticket or waiting on the CRM's engineering roadmap.
• An e-commerce platform lets each merchant connect their own shipping carrier, payment processor, or
marketing tool directly from their storefront admin, instead of asking the platform's team for custom API
access.
• A helpdesk or support product embeds a marketplace of connectors (Slack, Microsoft Teams, SMS) so
support teams can route or escalate tickets to the channel they already use, all configured from inside the
helpdesk.
• A vertical SaaS product (property management, healthcare scheduling, etc.) lets customers sync data to their
own systems of record, such as accounting software, an EHR, or a data warehouse, through an embedded
connector. This satisfies enterprise buyers who need deep integration, without the vendor building a one-off
integration for each customer.
Your Objective
Build a customer-facing solution on Fastn: something an actual customer sees, receives, or interacts with directly,
not an internal ops tool. Every workflow should move data between the systems customers already trust (storefronts,
CRMs, support desks) and a surface customers actually use, such as a portal, a notification, a chat message, or a
channel they already follow. Judges will be looking for solutions a real business could hand to a real customer on
day one.
How the Tracks Work
Teams either pick one of the four tracks below or propose their own idea under the customer-facing theme. The
connectors named in each track are just examples of that category. Any connector in the same category is fair game.
Every track follows the same basic pattern: the customer visits your app and connects an account through a
ready-made connector, with no coding needed on their end, and your workflow takes it from there.

The Four Tracks
Track 01: CRM Sync Between Two CRMs
A customer connects two different CRM accounts using connectors embedded in your app. Once connected,
customer records created or changed in one CRM are automatically kept in sync with the other. No manual copying,
no exports.
What Participants Build
• A workflow that connects to two different CRMs using Fastn's embedded connectors.
• A “Connect your CRMs” page inside your app where the customer links both accounts by clicking Connect
and logging in. No coding required on their end.
• Logic that copies a customer record (a contact, a deal, a company) from one CRM to the other, and updates it
whenever it changes.
• A simple status indicator so the customer can see that their accounts are connected and syncing.
Where to Embed the Connector
• An “Integrations” or “Connect your CRM” page inside your app's own settings, where the customer picks
and connects both CRMs themselves.
How the Customer Interacts With It
• The customer visits your app, clicks Connect next to each CRM, logs in once, and grants access.
• After that, any change made in one CRM shows up in the other automatically. The customer never has to
open either CRM and copy data by hand.
Example connectors: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive
Triggers: App event, Schedule
Judged on: How easy the connection process is for a non-technical customer, whether the sync is accurate, and how
clearly the app shows the customer that their data is in sync.
Track 02: Ecommerce Inventory and Order Sync
A store owner connects their storefront to your app. Your workflow keeps inventory counts and order status accurate
in real time, updating them the moment something changes.
What Participants Build
• A workflow that connects to an ecommerce platform using Fastn's embedded connector.
• A “Connect your store” step inside your app where the customer (a merchant) links their store account.
• Logic that updates stock levels or order status wherever they need to appear (a second store, a spreadsheet, an
internal tool) the moment they change upstream.
• Basic error handling, so a failed update gets logged instead of silently disappearing.
Where to Embed the Connector
• A “Connect your store” step inside your app's onboarding flow or settings page.

How the Customer Interacts With It
• The merchant clicks Connect Store, logs into their ecommerce account once, and selects what to sync.
• After that, inventory and order updates happen automatically in the background. The merchant just sees
accurate numbers whenever they check.
Example connectors: Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Stripe
Triggers: Webhook, App event
Judged on: How reliably inventory and orders stay in sync, how simple the connection flow is for the merchant, and
how clearly failures are surfaced instead of dropped.
Track 03: Subscribing to Notifications
A customer chooses what events they want to be notified about, and picks where they want to receive them: text,
email, WhatsApp, Slack, or another destination. Everything is set up from inside your app.
What Participants Build
• A “Notifications” page inside your app where the customer selects which events they want to hear about and
connects a destination (phone number, email, Slack, etc.) using Fastn's embedded connectors.
• A workflow that sends a notification to each customer's chosen destination when the relevant event happens.
• A way for the customer to update their preferences or disconnect a destination at any time.
Where to Embed the Connector
• A “Notifications” tab inside the customer's account settings, where they connect one or more destinations
themselves.
How the Customer Interacts With It
• The customer visits your app, turns on the notifications they care about, and connects where they want them
sent.
• After that, they receive updates on that channel automatically, with nothing else for them to do.
Example connectors: Twilio (SMS/WhatsApp), Gmail, Outlook, SendGrid, Slack, Microsoft Teams
Triggers: App event, Webhook, Schedule
Judged on: How easy it is for a customer to subscribe and pick a destination, whether notifications arrive reliably,
and whether customers can manage their own preferences without help.
Track 04: Business Intelligence Export
A customer connects their own reporting or data tool inside your app. Your workflow generates business data and
automatically exports it to the connected destination. No manual exports, no asking your team for a report.
What Participants Build
• A “Reporting” or “Export data” page inside your app where the customer connects their BI destination using
Fastn's embedded connector.
• A workflow that gathers business data (sales, usage, orders, or whatever fits your idea) and exports it to the
connected destination on a schedule or when a specific event happens.

• A simple confirmation the customer can see, showing that their data was exported successfully.
Where to Embed the Connector
• A “Reporting” or “Export data” page inside your app where the customer connects their destination and
chooses what to export.
How the Customer Interacts With It
• The customer visits your app, connects their destination once, and chooses what data they want exported.
• After that, their data shows up automatically, on schedule, with nothing else for them to do.
Example connectors: Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion
Triggers: Schedule, App event
Judged on: How clean and useful the exported data is, how simple the connection process is for the customer, and
whether exports run reliably on schedule.
